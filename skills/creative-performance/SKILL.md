# Creative performance

Read ads within a grounded parent and request ad-level reports for equal windows.
Compare spend, delivery, clicks, conversions, and value only when those metrics are
available under the same attribution basis. Use the analysis delegate for rankings or
contribution calculations over several ads.

The current active toolset sees ad entities and performance, not video hooks, text,
identity, review details, or material-level Smart Plus reports. Therefore identify an
ad as a performance lead, not a proven creative cause. Recommend the specific creative
evidence needed next; do not invent a hook, fatigue diagnosis, or replacement asset.
