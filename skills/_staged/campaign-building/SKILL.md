# Campaign building

This workflow remains staged until typed prerequisite reads and a campaign-bundle
draft exist. TikTok campaign creation is not one generic form: objective, promotion
type, app or website destination, placements, schedule, optimization goal, billing
event, bid, audience, identity, and creative must be validated together. A future
implementation must create a reviewable draft and may not publish or enable it from
the model turn.
