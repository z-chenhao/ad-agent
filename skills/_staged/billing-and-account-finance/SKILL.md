# Billing and account finance

This workflow remains staged until balance and transaction reads are typed. Report
currency, available balance, account budget, recent debits or credits, and estimated
runway from the same account and time basis. Financial records are sensitive and must
be minimized in model context. Transfers, billing-group changes, payment methods, and
account ownership are outside the agent's authority.
