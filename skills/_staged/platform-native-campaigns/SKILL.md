# Platform-native campaigns

Smart Plus and GMV Max are not legacy auction campaign aliases. Their materials,
identity/store authorization, optimization, reporting, and lifecycle contracts must be
modeled natively. This skill remains staged until typed reads, drafts, approval rules,
and controlled platform validation exist. Do not emulate these operations with the
conventional campaign tools.
