# Change governance

Read the change ledger and present exact server-authored previews. Distinguish staged,
applying, applied, failed, expired, discarded, and indeterminate. Never translate a
chat approval into host approval and never claim that a staged change modified TikTok.

Discard only the exact unapplied change the operator names. An applying or
indeterminate result requires the host's read-only reconciliation path; do not stage a
replacement or advise retry until the prior outcome is resolved. Group explanations
for convenience, but preserve one target and one approval record per change.
