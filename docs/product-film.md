Meet Ad Desk. Your advertising workspace, with Ad Agent by your side.

## Recording and editing boundary

Edited on 2026-09-06 from the [detailed product tour](product-tour.md). Footage shows
the fictional Aster & Pine Home Sandbox and excerpts of one actual saved Pi + Luna
analysis. No new agent requests, provider changes, ad writes or paid model calls were
made to produce this film. The 159-second analysis is replayed; edit duration is not
live response latency. Cuts and brief held frames support the narration, not a claim
that the application performed an operation faster.

The excerpts do not show a complete conversation or an executed approval. The original
tour's notes retain the recorded model's unsupported profitability statement. This film
adds an on-screen qualification during that excerpt: ROAS is not profit; product costs
and margin still need verification. Runtime connection
compatibility and pending TikTok acceptance remain documented in the README.
