## Recording boundary

The model session was recorded on 2026-09-05 in a fresh, isolated local Sandbox using
the fictional Aster & Pine Home advertiser; the 4K tour was finalized on 2026-09-06.
The ad assets retain their
[existing credits and licenses](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

The Agent portion replays one actual completed Pi + ChatGPT OAuth + Luna request:
13 successful tool calls, including a read-only analysis delegate, two final result
cards and no staged or applied advertising changes. The original analysis took about
159 seconds; replay navigation is not a claim about live response latency. Rerecording
the higher-resolution tour sent no additional model requests. Authentication, private
logs and provider transcripts are excluded from the video and repository.

The recorded model used the word “profitable” from ROAS alone. That conclusion is not
established by the supplied data: costs and margin are absent. The video explicitly
qualifies it, and the performance-insights skill now distinguishes reported efficiency
from profitability. The original saved response is preserved, not rewritten for the
demo. The skill correction passed structural and application checks; it has not been
retested with another paid model call.

Settings are inspected without changing providers or credentials. The Changes page
explains the approval boundary; this read-only tour does not demonstrate executing a
write. TikTok platform acceptance remains pending; Sandbox evidence is not live delivery.
